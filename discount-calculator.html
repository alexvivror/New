How discounts work

A percent-off discount reduces the original price by a percentage. This calculator instantly shows you the final sale price and exactly how much money you save — perfect for shopping sales and comparing deals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the sale price?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage, then subtract that from the original price. Example: 20% off $120 = $120 − $24 = $96.

What does 50% off mean?

You pay half of the original price. For example, 50% off $80 means you pay $40.

How do I calculate a final price after multiple discounts?

Apply each discount sequentially. If a store takes 20% then an extra 10%, apply the 20% first, then 10% off the already-reduced price.
