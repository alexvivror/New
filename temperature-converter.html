Convert Fahrenheit, Celsius & Kelvin

Temperature scales are used differently around the world: Fahrenheit in the US, Celsius nearly everywhere else, and Kelvin in science. This converter switches between all three instantly.

Quick reference points

Water freezes at 32°F / 0°C and boils at 212°F / 100°C. Room temperature is about 72°F / 22°C. Keep these anchors in mind and rough conversions become easy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit value, multiply by 5, and divide by 9. Example: (72−32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 22.2°C.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9, divide by 5, and add 32. Example: 100 × 9 ÷ 5 + 32 = 212°F.

At what temperature are F and C equal?

−40°F and −40°C are the same temperature. It is the one point where the two scales meet.
