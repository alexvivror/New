Understanding total interest

Over a 30-year mortgage, you may pay nearly as much in interest as the home itself. This calculator shows your total interest so you can compare loan terms and decide between a 15-year and 30-year mortgage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a mortgage payment calculated?

Payments use the formula M = P[r(1+r)ⁿ]/[(1+r)ⁿ−1], where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of payments.

Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?

A 15-year mortgage has higher monthly payments but far less total interest. A 30-year mortgage has lower payments but you pay significantly more interest over time.

What does the payment include?

This calculator estimates principal and interest only. Your actual payment also includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, and possibly PMI.
