Frequently Asked Questions

How many feet are in a mile?

There are 5,280 feet in one mile, or about 1,609 meters.

How do I convert kilograms to pounds?

Multiply the weight in kilograms by 2.20462. For example, 10 kg ≈ 22.05 lb.

How many liters are in a gallon?

One US gallon equals 3.785 liters. One imperial (UK) gallon equals 4.546 liters.
