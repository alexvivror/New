Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard tip percentage in the US?

15–20% is typical for sit-down restaurant service. 18% is a common default, with 20% for great service and 15% for adequate service.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Most people tip on the pre-tax amount, but tipping on the post-tax total is also common and slightly more generous. Either is acceptable.

How do I split a bill between people?

Divide the total (bill + tip) by the number of people. Our calculator does this automatically.
