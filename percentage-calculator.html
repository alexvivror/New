What is a percentage?

A percentage is a fraction of 100, written with the % sign. It is used everywhere — from discounts and tips to interest rates and statistics. Our calculator handles the most common percentage questions in one tap.

Common percentage problems

The three most common questions are: What is X% of Y? What percentage of Y is X? And what is the percentage increase or decrease between two numbers? This tool answers all three instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what X% of Y is?

Multiply X by Y and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 15 × 200 ÷ 100 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase, negative is a decrease.

How do I convert a fraction to a percentage?

Divide the numerator by the denominator and multiply by 100. For example 3/4 = 0.75 × 100 = 75%.
