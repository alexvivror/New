Frequently Asked Questions

How is my loan payment calculated?

Payments use the standard amortization formula M = P[r(1+r)ⁿ]/[(1+r)ⁿ−1], where P is the principal, r is the monthly rate, and n is the number of months.

What is the difference between a loan and a mortgage?

A mortgage is a loan secured by real estate, typically for buying a home. A personal loan is usually unsecured and has a shorter term and higher rate.

How can I reduce total interest?

Pay more than the minimum each month, choose a shorter term, or shop for a lower rate. Extra payments go directly toward principal and cut interest dramatically.
